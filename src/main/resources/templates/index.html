⚙️ Process
How ChainVerify Works

A four-step decentralized verification process that makes counterfeiting impossible.

STEP 01
🏭
Manufacturer Registration
Verified manufacturers register each product on-chain with a unique cryptographic token at point of production.
STEP 02
🏷️
QR / NFC Tagging
Every authentic product is embedded with a tamper-proof QR or NFC tag linked to its immutable blockchain record.
STEP 03
📱
Consumer Scan
Buyers scan the tag using the ChainVerify app or web portal. The system queries the blockchain in real-time.
STEP 04
✅
Instant Verdict
Results show GENUINE, COUNTERFEIT, or PENDING with full chain history — block number, timestamp, and hash.
